Published Kentucky winter-cover guidance

UK Fruit Facts (November–December 2024) describes placing covers when average weekly air temperature stays below 50°F and plants are well into cold acclimation. Typical placement is mid-November through December; the UK plasticulture crop profile describes late November.

  • Look for flattened leaves and a dull green or gray-green cast.
  • Review the forecast, establishment, field access, and cover condition.
  • A first cool week can be followed by warm weather. Review subsequent conditions.
  • Monitor beneath covers; warm, wet conditions can favor disease.

The rolling seven-day calculation is our operational interpretation. UK does not specify seven consecutive daily means below 50°F or the precise averaging method. The streak is descriptive only.

Separate decisions: growth and cold protection

Late / undersized plantings: temporary fall covers can increase heat accumulation. NC State advises considering cultivar, planting date and accumulated / forecast growing degree days; its late-planting guide discusses lightweight covers for two to three weeks. This tool does not calculate a cultivar-specific growth recommendation.

Damaging cold: do not wait for the weekly cue or a seven-day streak. NC State winter guidance calls for row-cover protection below 20°F, but injury risk varies with acclimation, cultivar and field conditions. This is regional guidance, not a universal injury threshold. The forecast table shows model lows for review; no background notification is sent by this page.

Methods, sources & alerts

Observed mean uses the Mesonet TAIR Avg_Val, converted from °C to °F. High/low midpoint uses (daily maximum + minimum) / 2, matching the calculation used in the Mesonet monthly-summary display. The weekly value is the equal-weight average of seven daily values. The primary weekly cue uses strictly below 50°F, matching the wording of UK guidance; the descriptive streak uses daily means at or below 50°F. Tests use unrounded values. Historical projections use the median first-cue date among complete prior seasons that reached it, mapped to the review year. The earliest–latest range describes observed variation, not a prediction interval. Ongoing seasons and gaps are excluded; the estimate does not incorporate forecasts or adjust for current warmth.

Days follow the selected station's local time, including daylight saving. Today is excluded. A day must have a valid source flag and at least 90% observation coverage; missing or invalid days break streaks and prevent a seven-day result. This 90% rule is a tool design choice. Historical review uses all years exposed by each station’s local-day data manifest.

Data reload when this page opens or you press Refresh. This page does not send background notifications. Background alerts require a separate scheduler and are not included in this code.
